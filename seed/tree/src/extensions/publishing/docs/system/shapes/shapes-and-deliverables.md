# Shapes And Deliverables

## Content Classes

* Everything that can be published is one of five content classes: `video`, `image`, `audio`, `prose` (a block document in the shell's model) and `file`. The classes are fixed in this extension; a shape's parts and a kind's slots are both typed by them, and that is what makes an assignment decidable.
* A `video` carries a duration; an `image` its dimensions; `prose` a body document; every class an optional transcript or alt text and a required synthetic-media disclosure, gathered once at production time rather than per channel.
- `CONTENT_CLASSES` in `lib/content-index.ts` names them; a website's slots are parsed against the same list.

## Shapes Are The Author's Vocabulary

* A **shape** is a deliverable type the author defines — *Episode*, *Statement*, *Weekly edition* — composed of **parts**. A part names a content class, a cardinality, a role label, and the constraints the author chooses to require at production time (aspect, duration, dimensions, formats). A shape may hold other shapes in order, so a *Series* holds *Episode*s.
* Shapes and parts are this extension's concept and types; which shapes exist is data. `calliopa-show` installs the *Episode* shape — `complete-episode`, two `main-video`s, `teaser`, `crossover`, `blooper`, four `cinematic-image`s, `statement` (image pages at 4:5 plus a PDF), `image-teaser`, `field-note` — and the *Series* shape, as data on top of this extension. User decision, 2026-09-14.

## Deliverables, Items, Renditions

* A **deliverable** is an instance of a shape, holding **items** that fill its parts. An **item** is one piece of content: its content class, its machine facts, a label, its disclosure, and either its **renditions** — one export each, a CCGW blob reference with its dimensions, aspect derived from them, and how it came to exist — or, for prose, its body document.
* An item may be held by several deliverables or by none; one held by none is standing, identified by what holds it rather than by a field. Nothing on an item says where it went: that is the log's answer ([Publishing](../publishing/publishing.md)).
* The smallest unit this extension publishes is one item to one slot of one channel. A shape is composition on top of that, and an instance without `calliopa-show` still shapes its own deliverables or publishes single items.
- Producing an item creates no obligation to publish it; the inventory is allowed to go unused.

## Vocabulary

- The vocabulary is declared as this extension's `ext.blocktype` and `ext.relationtype` members and validated by CCGW on every write, and each type arrives with the change that first writes it, so nothing is declared ahead of a writer. `PU_0001` declared `channel` (`id`, `kind`, `title`), `contentIndex` (`id`, `channel`, `readAt`; `containers`, `slots`, `copy` as the stored index's lists) and the relation `declares` (channel to contentIndex, at most one active per channel). The repository's kernel harness seeds the same three from `internal/kernel/serve/testdata/ui-shell-vocabulary.json` beside a `publishing` manifest, which is what makes Validation enforce them in the scratch graph (`PU_0001_002`).
- Type names are global across extensions — a member's node id is `node:<name>` and Validation's definition set is one set — and `calliopa-video`'s members stand in the graph while that extension does. This extension's names therefore never reuse `calliopa-video`'s (`asset`, `rendition`, `publication`, `holds`, `exports`, `body`, …) until `calliopa-show` supersedes it and its members are deleted.

## The Work Layer

Under `PU_0002`, requested 2026-09-14, the second of the sketched changes: the shapes the author defines, deliverables as their instances, items with their exports, and ingest, so there is something to assign and publish in `PU_0003`. Nothing publishes here. `calliopa-video`'s production model is the source — an asset's three independent axes, an asset held by several episodes or by none, the teaser chosen by name, a prose body as a shell document, bytes as CCGW blobs, machine facts read off the file at ingest — with its nine roles and its serial left to `calliopa-show`.

* A shape is created from the library, named, and composed of parts. A part names a content class, a cardinality — exactly one, at most one, one or more, any number — a role label, and the constraints the author requires at production time: aspect, a duration limit, dimensions, formats. A shape may hold other shapes in order, so a deliverable of the outer shape gathers deliverables of the inner one.
* A deliverable is created from the library by choosing its shape, titled, and filled part by part. A part's cardinality and constraints are checked where an item is placed; an item that does not fit is refused naming the part's rule, and the item itself stays free to fit elsewhere.
* An item carries its class, its machine facts — a video's duration, an image's dimensions — an optional transcript and alt text, a label, and a required synthetic-media disclosure; and either its exports, one per rendition, each a CCGW blob with its dimensions and how it came to exist, or, for prose, its body document in the shell's block model.
* Ingest is from a file chosen or dropped in the browser: the facts are read off the bytes before anything is written, the blob uploaded immediately before the export that references it, and a file whose facts cannot be read is refused with nothing stored. Exports are content-addressed, so the same bytes twice are one object and a retry changes nothing.
* An item gathered by no deliverable is standing, listed by the library and identified by what gathers it rather than by a field.
- The blob store's cap (`BLOB_MAX_BYTES`, 100 MiB by default) bounds what one upload from the browser can be; a complete episode at 180 s does not fit. An upload above the cap is refused naming it; a streamed or resumable upload through the kernel arrives with `BO_0252`'s upload half.

- A part is filled two ways: an upload into it creates the item and places it in one gesture, and *Add existing* picks from the items of its class, so a crossover shared by two episodes is one item gathered twice. User decision, 2026-09-14.
- A prose item's body is a shell `document`, created with the item and opened in the editor; whether the Documents category lists it is the shell's rule. User decision, 2026-09-14.
- The library shows one `Deliverables` category grouped by top-level shape; a category per shape is what `calliopa-show` may contribute for its own. User decision, 2026-09-14.
- A part's constraint refuses at placement: an item that violates it is refused naming the rule, and stays free to fit elsewhere. User decision, 2026-09-14.
- A file above the blob store's cap is refused naming the cap; a streamed or resumable upload through the kernel is `BO_0252`'s upload half, out of scope here. User decision, 2026-09-14.

- The vocabulary is declared as this extension's members and seeded in the kernel harness fixture beside the three `PU_0001` declared: `shape` (`title`), `part` (`title`, `class` one of the five content classes or `shape`, `cardinality` one of `one`, `optional`, `some`, `any`, `role`, `constraints`, `order` as a fractional key, `shape` for a nested part), `deliverable` (`title`), `item` (`class`, `label`, `synthetic`, `durationSeconds`, `width`, `height`, `transcript`, `alt`), `export` (`bytes` as the top-level blob reference carrying the filename it arrived as, `mediaType`, `size`, `width`, `height`, `provenance` one of `ingested`, `produced`); relations `composes` (shape to part), `nests` (shape to shape), `shaped` (deliverable to shape), `gathers` (deliverable to item, and deliverable to deliverable for a nested shape), `fills` (item or deliverable to part), `exported` (item to export), `prose` (item to the shell's `document`). None is `contains`, and none reuses `calliopa-video`'s names. `server/vocabulary.ts` names them beside the pure rules (`PU_0002_001`).
- `server/shapes.ts` holds shapes and parts over the graph: `createShape`, `retitleShape`, `deleteShape` (refused while a deliverable is shaped by it, `shapeInUse`, or another shape nests it, `shapeNested`; retires the parts with it), `addPart` at the end of the order (refused in words by `partRefusal` — a blank title, a class or cardinality outside the vocabulary, a nested part without a shape, a constraint outside the vocabulary — and `nestsItself` for a shape nesting itself directly or through what the inner shape already nests, walked through `nests`), `revisePart` (title, cardinality, role, constraints; class and nested shape fixed once made), `reorderPart` by a fractional key before another or last, `removePart` (refused while an item fills it, `partFilled`). A nested part carries `shape` and relates the outer shape to the inner by `nests` in the same script (`PU_0002_002`).
- `server/deliverables.ts` holds deliverables and placement: `createDeliverable` from a shape the graph holds, `retitleDeliverable`, `deleteDeliverable` (what it gathered stays; the log's refusal arrives with `PU_0003`), `placeItem` — `gathers` and `fills` in one script after `placementRefusal` in `server/vocabulary.ts` has checked the part's class, its cardinality against what already fills it in this deliverable, and its constraints against the item's facts and exports (one export at the aspect, the duration within the limit, one export of at least the dimensions, one export in the formats), refused as `doesNotFit` naming the rule — `placeDeliverable` for a part of class `shape` (the inner deliverable's shape must be the part's; a deliverable never gathers itself), and `release`, which closes both relations and deletes nothing. `readDeliverable` is two rooted reads — the deliverable, then its shape and everything it gathers — and answers the parts with what fills them and the items of each class that could, for *Add existing* (`PU_0002_003`).
- `server/items.ts` holds items, exports and ingest: `ingestItem` names the class from the request or the media type, reads the facts off the bytes for a video (an ISO base media file) or an image (PNG or JPEG) through `server/media-facts.ts` — ported from `calliopa-video`'s reader, a format it does not know refused as `unreadableMedia`, a still offered as a video refused as `notAVideo` and the reverse as `notAnImage` — takes audio and a file as they are, refuses an empty file and one above the cap (`aboveTheCap`, naming the cap), puts the bytes to CCGW through the shell's blob client immediately before the item and its first export in one script, the export's `bytes` the blob reference; `addExport` adds a rendition or, with `replaces`, closes the item's `exported` relation to the old one and keeps it; `createProseItem` creates a shell document titled by the label and relates it by `prose`; `reviseItem` sets the label, alt text, transcript and disclosure, one write per statement; `deleteItem` retires the item with its exports and is refused while any deliverable gathers it (`itemGathered`). `listItems` answers every item with its export count and which of them nothing gathers, the standing ones (`PU_0002_004`).
- The library: `Shapes` is an item section whose `+` creates *Untitled shape* at once and opens it; `Deliverables` is `components/deliverables-section.tsx`, groups per top-level shape with the section's own `+` toggling a form — a shape chooser and a title — in the Extensions section's idiom, the listing read again as the form opens so a shape just made is offered (the shell re-reads the section on a deliverable tab's change, not a shape's); `Standing Items` is `components/standing-section.tsx`, the items nothing gathers with their class as the badge and a `+` that takes a file through the picker, refusing one above the cap before sending. Each has its reader in `contributions.server.ts` (`PU_0002_005`).
- The views: `views/shape.tsx` edits a shape's title and parts — add with class, cardinality, role and constraints or the nested shape, move up and down, remove — and offers *Delete shape* in the inspector with the part count and what nests it as facts; `views/deliverable.tsx` shows each part with what fills it, *Upload into <part>* for a media or file part, *Add existing* over the items of the part's class, *Release* per member, nested deliverables opening in their own tab, and *Delete deliverable*; `views/item.tsx` shows the class, facts and exports with a preview for an image — read through the shell's `/api/blobs/<objectId>`, since CCGW's own `/v1/blobs` is not reachable from the app origin and the preview drew nothing until `BO_0273_046` — *Replace* per export and *Add an export*, the label, alt text, transcript and disclosure editable in place, *Open the document* for prose, and *Delete item*. The handler table is `server/work-api.ts` under `/api/x/publishing/`: `shapes`, `shapes/[id]`, `shapes/[id]/parts`, `shapes/[id]/parts/[part]` and `/move`; `deliverables`, `deliverables/[id]`, `deliverables/[id]/parts/[part]/items`, `/upload`, `/deliverables` and `/members/[member]`; `items`, `items/standing`, `items/prose`, `items/[id]`, `items/[id]/exports`. An upload is the file's bytes as the request body with its media type and `x-filename` (`PU_0002_006`).
- `server/vocabulary.test.ts` proves the placement rules, what a part must be and the derived facts over plain values; `server/media-facts.test.ts` the readers over fixtures built to each format's specification; `components/deliverables-section.test.ts` presses both sections by name in Qwik's render harness; `tests/behavior/work.test.ts` proves the layer over the kernel harness with its blob store: parts added in order, revised, reordered and removed with every refusal, a nested shape and the cycle refused, a PNG and an MP4 ingested with their facts read and their bytes as referenced blobs, the same bytes twice as one object, a replacement export superseding the first, an unreadable and an empty file refused with nothing stored, a prose item's document, an item's words revised, placement where it fits and refused where it does not — class, cardinality, duration — release, nesting, the standing and grouped listings, and the deletes refused while something points at the deleted (`PU_0002_007`).
