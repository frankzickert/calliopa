# Focused Work

## Purpose

- This document is the authoritative description of focused work as a capability of the shell: opening a block of a view's target as its own work root, coming back along the route, and what a view draws for it.
- `CA_0065_FEAT_focused-work-is-a-shell-capability` is the originating change. `CA_0047` built the capability across `documents` and `calliopa-refine`; this change makes it the frame's.
- [Workspace View Types](./view-types.md) owns the view contract this extends. [Tabs](./tabs.md) owns the route, the retarget and the workspace record, which are already the shell's and do not move.
- What each view draws for it is its own: `documents`' [Block Editor](../documents/block-editor.md), *Focused Work*, has the control's place on the block, the face's line and the pinch.

## What Focused Work Is

* Any block can be opened as focused work. The focused block becomes the current work root: the interface then shows an intent — its words — a body, a refinement state, evidence, questions, relations and a next move of its own, and returning to the containing work copies nothing: the parent renders the child's current synthesis as the block's face (`BO_0243`, the material's §8; `CA_0047`).
* The affordance is discoverable. A focused block's expansion ends with *Open as focused work →*, and on touch a pinch outward on a block — zooming in — does the same; back, or a pinch inward, returns to the containing work (user decision, 2026-09-14: the direction was inward-to-open until the walk on a phone). The reader never thinks *create a new document*. No gesture is the only path (the material's §42, §43).
* Navigation is route, not ontology: the route above the intent is the path by which the reader arrived and nothing else.
* Work drifts naturally: a block becomes independently focusable when it has accumulated enough of its own, and the reader simply opens it. No conceptual migration is asked of them.
- These four are the shell's since `CA_0065_006` (2026-09-23), unchanged in their words: they say what focused work is, which is the frame's to say now that the capability is.

## Why It Is The Shell's

- The whole effect of opening a block as focused work is on the frame: a tab is retargeted, a route is pushed, a block is landed on. Those are three things the shell owns and no view may do for itself ([View Types](./view-types.md), View Contract).
- Every view over every target kind has the same need — open one part of what I am reading as its own root, and come back. Offered by an extension, it reaches one view and costs any other view two imports.
- What made it urgent: both visible ways in are `calliopa-refine`'s, and `BO_0285` took refinement out of what a release carries on 2026-09-23. On a shipping install the write, the route and the retarget all work and nothing draws a way in; the only survivor is `documents`' pinch, on touch. A capability the frame provides cannot be switched off with an extension.
- `calliopa-refine`'s `server/gesture.ts` imports `documents`' `server/focus.ts` directly, noted as a technical decision under `RF_0005_002`. That import is the symptom: the capability has no home to be called from. An extension importing a shell host module under `~/server/` or `~/lib/` is ordinary and stays so.

## What Belongs Where

* **Focused work is a capability of the shell.** The child's creation, the `focuses` edge, the reads that answer a block's child and a target's faces, the retarget, the route and the landing are the frame's, offered to every view. User decision, 2026-09-23, reversing `BO_0262`'s decision of 2026-09-18 that it stays in `calliopa-refine`.
* **The shell asks the target's own extension for the child.** `document`, `text` and `contains` are `documents`' vocabulary and only `focuses` is the shell's, so the shell never writes another extension's types: the extension contributing a target kind contributes how a child of that kind is made, and the capability is general from the start rather than document-shaped. User decision, 2026-09-23, over the shell writing `documents`' vocabulary directly.
* **The shell contributes the control, the view renders it.** *Open as focused work* is a shell-contributed action in the view's own block row, in the named-action vocabulary the bar and the inspector already share, so every install has one way in and no extension can withhold it. The shell still draws nothing inside a view's content: the view declares the slot and renders the action through `ActionControl`. User decision, 2026-09-23.
* **The parent's face is the view's to draw, from a read the shell answers.** A block whose child holds a synthesis wears it as a muted line; what that line looks like is the view's, and the faces come from the shell as `focus` and `reveal` already do. User decision, 2026-09-23.
- What stays the view's besides: the gesture that means it — `documents`' pinch adapter over the shell's `src/lib/pinch.ts` — where the route line renders until `CA_0048` moves it into the frame, and what landing on a block means.
- What stays `calliopa-refine`'s: the depth icon row, its categories and the gestures beside them. What leaves refine is the capability, not the taste of how a block presents itself.
- Nothing about the model changes. A focused work is still a document that `focuses` a block, a block is focused by at most one document, the child is parentless in the library's sense, retiring a focused block is still refused, and the route stays navigation only, with no governance, ownership or containment meaning ([Block Document Model](../documents/block-document-model.md)).

## The Child Contribution

- An extension contributing a target kind contributes how a child of that kind is made and read (`CA_0065_001`, landed 2026-09-23): `FocusedWorkContribution` on `ServerContributions`, keyed by the bare kind, carrying `childType` — the node type a child is, which the shell reads the `focuses` edge to — `plan`, which answers the child a block would open as, and `faces`, which answers what each child says for itself. A target kind that contributes none opens no focused work, and the shell's control is not drawn for it.
- The plan is statements, not a write (`src/contract.ts`, `ChildPlan`): the child's identity, its title, the statements creating it and the parameters they read. The extension commits nothing; the shell folds the plan into its own script so the child and the edge are one write. Which of its blocks may be opened, and the refusal in words when one may not, are the extension's — `documents` refuses a block that is not in the target and a block that is not `text`.
- `buildServerRegistry` keys the contributions by the qualified kind, as a tab's kind is (`registry.ts`, `focusedWork`), so the shell reaches the contribution from the target it already holds; `focusedWorkFor` in `src/server/registry.ts` is the lookup. No collision is possible and none is refused: a kind is already namespaced by the extension contributing it. `src/registry.test.ts` proves the keying and that a kind contributing none answers nothing.

## The Capability

- The capability is `src/server/focused-work.ts` (`CA_0065_002`, landed 2026-09-23): `FOCUSES`, `childrenOf` — the rooted read over `focuses` seeded at the blocks — `facesOf`, `openFocusedWork` and `focusOf`, each taking the qualified target kind and reaching the contribution through it. A kind that contributes none is refused `focusedWorkUnsupported` in words. What it answers no longer names a document: `FocusedChild` is `itemId`, `title` and `face`, and `OpenedFocusedWork` is `itemId`.
- The child and the edge are one write. `focusScript` is pure — the extension's statements, then `RELATE fwChild -[f:focuses]-> fwBlock`, with the plan's parameters under the shell's two refs — so a child never stands without the edge that makes it focused work, and the composition is proven without a graph (`src/server/focused-work.test.ts`). The shell's `fwChild` and `fwBlock` are written last, so an extension that happened to use those names cannot redirect the edge.
- The contribution is resolved when it is needed, not when the module loads. Technical decision at implementation, 2026-09-23: the server registry imports every extension's server half, so a static import would close a cycle through any extension module that opens focused work — `documents`' own writes and `calliopa-refine`'s reach both do — and leave one extension's contributions undefined while the registry was still being built, which is exactly how `calliopa-refine`'s two route suites failed until it was made a call-time `await import`.
- The frame's own endpoint (`CA_0065_003`, landed 2026-09-23): `GET /api/focused-work/<item>?kind=<qualified kind>` answers the face of every block of the target that has focused work, and `POST` with `{ kind, blockId }` opens one block or answers the child that already focuses it. A view asks through the bridge and never through the extension that owns the target's vocabulary; the kind names which contribution answers. `documents`' own `openFocusedWork` command and its `focused` route are gone, and so is the entry `calliopa-refine` served with `documents`' handler.
- The bridge carries three things (`CA_0065_003`, `CA_0065_004`, `CA_0065_005`): `pressBlockControl$`, which acts and answers a refusal in words or `null`; `blockControls$`, which answers what the frame offers on one block; and `faces$`, which answers what a target's blocks have. The view names its target and its block and never a kind — the shell reads the kind from the tab the target is open in (`kindOf` in `src/lib/tabs.ts`), so a vocabulary stays out of a view's reach. `retarget$` keeps doing what it does for *Back* and the crumbs ([View Types](./view-types.md)).
- The shell keeps the faces a view read, by target, so a control's words can say whether the block already has a child without a read per block. A block focused before any face was read is offered *Open as focused work*; a view reads the faces on its first block focus, so the words are right by the time a reader sees them. Technical decision at implementation, 2026-09-23.
- The contract now carries one thing the other way (`CA_0065_004`, landed 2026-09-23). `BlockControl` is what the shell offers on a block — an id, the words and an icon, and no handler: the view wraps it in a `ViewAction` whose `run$` calls `pressBlockControl$` and renders it through `ActionControl`, so the shell mints no QRL at runtime and the one named-action vocabulary still covers every surface. `ActionSurface` gained `"block"` beside `"inspector"` and `"bar"`, and a control drawn there carries `data-block-action`.
* A view says where the shell's controls are drawn and nothing else. It may not change a control's words, its order among the shell's, or whether it is offered: a capability the frame provides is not an extension's to withhold, which is the whole reason focused work moved. User decision, 2026-09-23.
- The faces are a call, not a store (`CA_0065_005`, landed 2026-09-23): `faces$(itemId)` answers each of the target's blocks that has a child, with the child's identity, its title and its own words when it holds any. `focus` and `reveal` are one-shot signals the shell writes; what a target's blocks have opened is data a view asks for when it first needs it, so a call is the shape and the read stays off the document read. Technical decision at implementation, revising the store this task first named.

## What Moves, And From Where

- The move landed whole (`CA_0065_006`, 2026-09-23): `documents` draws the control, the face and the pinch and contributes the child; `calliopa-refine` drew neither afterwards and reaches the shell for the child; and the four fixed product lines stand above, unchanged, with `documents`' *Focused Work* keeping what it itself draws.
- Technical decision, 2026-09-23: the behaviour suite moves to the tree root's `tests/behavior/` beside the shell's others. The kernel harness runs `vitest run --project behavior` over the whole project rather than a named list (`internal/kernel/serve/shell_documents_verification_test.go`), so no fixed-layer change follows and `CA_0065` has no `BO` half.
- Technical decision, 2026-09-23: the pinch adapter stays each view's. `src/lib/pinch.ts` is already the shell's and pure; a view that wants the gesture installs the adapter and calls `openFocusedWork$`, as `documents` does today.
